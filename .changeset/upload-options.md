`attachments.upload()` can now send the fields the upload route validates. Resize (form upload only): `width`, `height`, `fit`, `sizeName` (sent as `w`, `h`, `fit`, `size_name`) and `asTemp`. Every strategy: `replaceOld`, `title`, `caption`, `tempTimeoutMinutes`, `tempTimeoutAt` and `folderId`, which moves up from `DirectUploadOptions` to `UploadOptions`. When the server would silently ignore or override an option, the client throws `unencodable_value` before any request: `asTemp: true` with a `private` / `public` visibility, `asTemp: false` with a `temp_*` visibility, a timeout without a temporary upload, both timeouts, `replaceOld: true` without `recordType` and `recordId`, `sizeName` together with `width`, `height` or `fit`, and a `width` or `height` that is not a positive integer. Only a truthy flag counts: `replaceOld: false` is not sent. `replaceOld` replaces what is in that `collection` (`default` when omitted) of the record and deletes old files nothing else links to. `tempTimeoutAt` is sent as given (a `Date` as ISO); the server drops the offset, so a UTC string is only right when the host's `app.timezone` is UTC. The server's own ranges (width and height 10 to 3000, title length, timeout cap) are not copied: the host's 422 reports them.
